This Cookie Notice explains how Hanzo AI, Inc. uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage, SDKs, pixels) on hanzo.ai and the Hanzo Console. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. Categories

- **Strictly necessary.** Authentication, session integrity, security, load balancing, and core functionality. These are always active; the Services cannot operate without them, so they do not require consent.
- **Preferences.** Remember your settings, such as theme and language. Off until enabled where consent is required.
- **Analytics.** Aggregate, privacy-respecting measurement of feature usage and reliability. Off until consent where consent is required.
- **Marketing.** Measuring campaigns and communications. Hanzo does **not** currently use cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising or sell/share data through advertising cookies. If this ever changes, we will update this Notice in advance, provide the required opt-out, and honor Global Privacy Control.

## 2. Consent and control

Where law requires consent, nonessential cookies remain **disabled until you consent**, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time through **Cookie Settings** (`[COOKIE SETTINGS LINK — REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION]`). You can also control cookies through your browser. We honor recognized universal opt-out signals, including Global Privacy Control, for the purposes covered by applicable U.S. law.

A browser privacy signal governs cookies and comparable tracking; it does not by itself withdraw a separate, affirmative [research contribution](/legal/research-contribution), which is managed independently in your settings.

## 3. Retention

Nonessential analytics identifiers are retained no longer than the period listed in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) retention schedule — generally no more than 13 months — after which they expire or are renewed only with continued consent. Strictly necessary cookies persist only as long as the session or security function requires.
