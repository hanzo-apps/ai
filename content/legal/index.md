This is the canonical home for the agreements, policies, and notices that govern the Hanzo Services. Every Hanzo surface — the Console, cloud, API documentation, and signup — links to these documents at `hanzo.ai/legal/<name>` rather than restating them, so there is one authoritative version of each.

> **Status: draft, pending counsel.** The documents below are complete drafts written to be truthful about what the platform does today. They are **not yet effective**. Each carries a publication gate listing what must be verified — entity address, mailboxes, retention jobs, subprocessor rows, consent pipeline — before it is published as binding. See the internal implementation checklist for the engineering gates behind these commitments.

## Agreements

- **[Terms of Service](/legal/terms)** — the master agreement for all Services, including Enso routing, benchmarks, research, agents, and APIs.
- **[Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa)** — processor terms, SCCs, subprocessor governance, and security measures for business customers.

## Policies

- **[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy)** — what personal data we process, why, and the four data-use states that govern research and training.
- **[Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use)** — the conduct rules for every user, application, and agent.
- **[AI Research and Data Contribution Terms](/legal/research-contribution)** — the opt-in terms for using your data in Hanzo research and training, and the Verified Commons.
- **[Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/consumer-health)** — our posture under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws.

## Notices and registers

- **[Research API Supplemental Terms](/legal/research-api)** — terms for the `/v1/research` registry, evidence integrity, and durability states.
- **[Subprocessor Register](/legal/subprocessors)** — the providers that process data on your behalf, including the Enso routing chain.
- **[Cookie Notice](/legal/cookies)** — cookies and similar technologies, and how to control them.
- **[Biometric, Voice, and Likeness Notice](/legal/biometric-voice)** — how features that process faces, voices, or likeness are governed.

## How these fit together

The **Terms of Service** is the spine; it incorporates the **Acceptable Use Policy**, **Privacy Policy**, **DPA**, and — only on an affirmative election — the **AI Research and Data Contribution Terms**. The Privacy Policy defines four data-use states (**service-only** by default, then **private improvement**, **research and training**, and **public commons**); no data is used for training without an affirmative election, and none is published without a separate per-artifact contribution. The **Subprocessor Register** names every provider a request can reach, including model providers behind an inference gateway. The remaining notices cover cookies, biometric/voice/likeness, consumer health, and the research registry.
